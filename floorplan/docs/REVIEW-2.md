### SEAM-1 — Capture review shows RoomQuality wall/corner numbers in outline order, but the wall list numbers walls in capture order

- **Severity:** major (reviewer: major) · **Verdict:** confirmed
- **Where:** `floorplan/app/src/main/java/com/oroplex/floorplan/ui/capture/ReviewPanel.kt:149`

- **Problem:** RoomQuality.assess numbers walls by the solved outline's edge order (core/.../solve/RoomQuality.kt:88-89, wallNumber at :144) and corners by outline corner order (:203). ReviewPanel prints those reasons verbatim (ReviewPanel.kt:147-150). The capture wall/corner chips and re-capture actions use the capture index (CaptureControls.kt:405-420), and the solver's own warnings in the same list also use capture numbers (RoomSolver.kt:186-188, 219, 576, 1061). The two numberings differ whenever the solver reverses or re-orders the outline. For a clockwise walk, normalizeCcw reverses the edges and startAtEarliestCapture rotates them (RoomSolver.kt:953-970, reverseOutline :380). Capture walls [1,2,3,4] become outline edges [1,4,3,2]. Merged duplicates, connectors and dropped corner taps shift the numbers further. SolveResult.edgeSources already gives the mapping, but nothing uses it. The same mismatch exists in the editor in reverse: solver warnings, which FloorChecks now also shows in the floor check and the pre-export card, use capture numbers, while the wall panel says "Wall N of M" in outline order (EditorPanels.kt:221).
- **Failure scenario:** The user walks the room turning right, a clockwise capture of 4 walls. Capture wall 4 is noisy (RMS 3 cm). REVIEW shows "Wall 2 was noisy (±3.0 cm) — consider recapturing". The user taps Back to walls, picks chip 2 and re-captures a wall that was fine. The noisy wall stays, and the review still says "Wall 2 ...". Corners mode has the same problem with "Corner N is X from where you tapped". About half of all users walk clockwise.
- **Suggested fix:** Give RoomQuality a way to number walls by capture order: pass SolveResult.edgeSources, or an optional per-edge label mapping, and use capture numbers in the capture review. Keep outline numbers in the editor. In the editor, translate solver warnings to outline numbers, or stop showing wall numbers in stored warnings. Also add a test with a clockwise capture.

- **Verifier:** RoomQuality.wallsScore numbers walls by the stored outline (wallNumber(k) = k+1 when room.corners is already CCW). cornersScore does the same by outline corner. ReviewPanel prints those reasons for ui.draftRoom, and draftRoom's corners are the solver's outline (RoomDraft.toRoom: corners = solved.corners). The capture chips (CaptureControls CaptureList: "Wall ${i+1}" over d.observations), the AR overlay badges (CaptureOverlay:125, "${i + 1}") and the solver's own warnings (RoomSolver:219, 576, 1061, wallsHint) all use capture indices. I worked through a clockwise capture: buildOutline gives edges [w0,w1,w2,w3], and the signed area is negative, so normalizeCcw calls reverseOutline. New edge k carries old edge (n-k)%n, giving [w0,w3,w2,w1]. startAtEarliestCapture leaves w0 at index 0. So capture wall 4 is outline edge 1 and is reported as "Wall 2", exactly as claimed. Corners mode has the same problem: a CW tap order [t0..t3] gives outline corners [t1,t0,t3,t2], so "Corner 1" is tap 2. Nothing in :app uses SolveResult.edgeSources (grep finds no use). The only clockwise test (RoomQualityTest.clockwiseOutlinesKeepTheirWallNumbers) covers a stored CW outline, not capture order. The chip colours do not tell the user which wall is meant: wallGrade only marks a wall POOR above 5 cm RMS (MAX_FAIR_RMS), while RoomQuality calls it noisy above 2.5 cm, so a wall with 3 cm RMS can show a GOOD dot on chip 4 while the text names wall 2. The reverse mismatch in the editor is also real: Room.warnings carry capture numbers and are shown in RoomPanel and in FloorChecks ("Name: <warning>"), next to "Wall N of M" (EditorPanels:221) and RoomQuality reasons in outline numbering (FloorChecks:73). I am keeping major: about half of all scans will steer the user to re-capture the wrong wall, in the quality-feedback feature added in round 2. No data is corrupted.

- **Fix notes:** core RoomQuality.assess: add an additive parameter, e.g. `numbering: WallNumbering = WallNumbering.OUTLINE` (or `captureNumbers: Boolean = false`). In wallsScore, keep each observation's ORIGINAL index into room.observations (filter with mapIndexedNotNull, not filter) and record on each EdgeGrade the index of the observation that gave its best score. In CAPTURE mode, name noisy and weak walls by that observation index + 1, which is the capture to redo and matches chips and AR badges. Unsupported edges have no capture, so word them by their neighbours, e.g. "The wall between walls 3 and 4 wasn't measured directly", using the neighbouring edges' best-observation numbers. In cornersScore, name a far corner by the index + 1 of its nearest tap in room.cornerTaps (original index). app ReviewPanel / CaptureQuality.safeAssess: call assess(room, CAPTURE). Leave the editor, FloorChecks and export on OUTLINE. Editor side: make the stored solver warnings use outline numbers. Either have RoomSolver build both lists (SolveResult gains an additive `outlineWarnings`, mapping capture index to outline edge via the inverse of edgeSources and falling back to "capture N" for lost or merged captures), with CaptureViewModel.toRoom (at save) and RoomOps.resolve storing outlineWarnings, or stop putting wall numbers in stored warnings. Tests: a clockwise 4-wall capture with capture 4 noisy, where review says "Wall 4" and the editor's reason says "Wall 2". Also a CW corner capture.


### SEAM-2 — Session/floor axis hint is the non-robust 4θ mean, so one angled wall tilts every later room in the session by about 1°

- **Severity:** major (reviewer: major) · **Verdict:** confirmed
- **Where:** `floorplan/app/src/main/java/com/oroplex/floorplan/ui/capture/SessionGeometry.kt:60`

- **Problem:** sessionAxisHint returns RoomSolver.dominantAngle, a plain length-weighted 4θ mean, over every edge of the session's saved rooms (SessionGeometry.kt:52-60). The editor's re-solve uses the same plain mean in floorAxisHint (EditorGeometry.kt:506-513), without even the coherence check. Core's own FloorAxis documents that this mean is pulled by angled walls (FloorAxis.kt:113-116, about 1° for a 3 m wall at 30°). The robust estimator is `internal` to :core, so the app could not call it. RoomSolver.withHint then adopts the hint whenever the room's own robust axis is within squareToleranceDeg (5°) of it (RoomSolver.kt:352-356). The Snapper rotates every square wall of the new room onto the biased axis. The result is that round-1 product AR-7 (shared walls not parallel) is back, as a systematic error. It is only fixed for sessions where every wall is square or at exactly 45°.
- **Failure scenario:** Room 1 is 4×5 m with a 1.5 m wall at 30° (a bay or chamfer). Its own robust axis squares its walls exactly to θ0. The hint for room 2 is θ0 + 1.18° (computed: atan2(1.5·sin120°, 16.5 + 1.5·cos120°)/4), with coherence 0.88, which passes. Room 2's own axis is about θ0, within 5° of the hint, so it is squared to θ0 + 1.18°. The shared partition is drawn as a wedge that diverges about 8 cm over 4 m, and overall dimensions are skewed. In the editor, re-solving a main-session room on a floor that also holds an unsnapped later-session room at a 12° heading gives a hint of about 3° (40 m at 0° plus 14 m at 12°). The re-solved room rotates 3° off its neighbours, although the dialog promises it "comes out parallel to its neighbours".
- **Suggested fix:** Make FloorAxis public, or add a public RoomSolver.robustFloorAxis, and use it for both hints. Better still, carry the squaring axis actually used (SolveResult.dominantAngle of the session's first room) in CaptureUiState and pass that. In the editor, compute the hint only from rooms in the same session, or from rooms aligned with this one, and apply the coherence check.

- **Verifier:** sessionAxisHint (SessionGeometry.kt:35-61) is a plain length-weighted 4θ mean (RoomSolver.dominantAngle) over every edge of ui.sessionRooms, with only a coherence check. FloorAxis, whose docs say this mean is biased by angled walls, is `internal object` in :core, and Kotlin internal is module-scoped. The only public users are FloorOps.straighten and the renderer, so the app had no robust estimator to call. I reproduced the numbers: 16.5 m of square walls at θ0 plus 1.5 m at 30° (4θ = 120°) gives c = 16.5 - 0.75 = 15.75 and s = 1.299, so the hint is atan2(1.299, 15.75)/4 = 1.18°, with coherence 15.80/18 = 0.878 > 0.3. withHint (RoomSolver:352-356) adopts the hint whenever the room's own robustAxis is within squareToleranceDeg (5°). Room 2 is therefore squared to θ0 + 1.18°, which gives tan(1.18°)·4 m ≈ 8 cm of wedge on a shared 4 m wall. Later rooms stay at about θ0 + 1.18° (their own edges reinforce the hint), so the room with the angled wall stays misaligned with all the others. The same happens for any wall the solver leaves unsquared: a 10° skewed 4 m wall among 14 m of square walls gives about 2.1°. The editor's floorAxisHint (EditorGeometry.kt:506-513) is worse: it uses every other room on the floor (other sessions, MANUAL, unsnapped) with no coherence check. 40 m at 0° plus 14 m at 12° gives c = 49.37, s = 10.40, so the hint is 2.97°, adopted, and PlanEditorViewModel.resolveRoom rotates the re-solved room about 3° off its same-session neighbours. Only sessions where every wall is square or exactly 45° (sin 4θ = 0) are unaffected. The error depends on these conditions, but it is systematic and it brings back the round-1 problem exactly where axisHint was meant to fix it.

- **Fix notes:** core: expose a robust shared axis, for example a public `RoomSolver.sharedAxis(outlines: List<List<Vec2>>, toleranceDeg: Double = 5.0, minSupport: Double = 0.5): Double?`. Implement it as robustAxis over all edges (support = min(length, cap such as 3 m), precision = length) and return null when the supporting length is below minSupport of the total. Alternatively make FloorAxis public, but consensus seeding is safer than FloorAxis's mean-then-window refinement. Capture (better): add `sessionAxis: Double?` to CaptureUiState. Set it when the first room of a session is saved, from that draft's SolveResult.dominantAngle (the axis actually used), and pass it to solve() for later rooms instead of sessionAxisHint(s.sessionRooms). Clear it wherever sessionRoomIds are reset (attach() with a new sessionId, process restore). Fall back to the robust sharedAxis over sessionRooms when it is unknown. Note that anchor corrections at finish rotate rooms slightly, which does not matter during capture. Editor floorAxisHint: use only rooms with the same non-null sessionId as the room being re-solved (they share its frame), through the robust estimator. Return null for MANUAL rooms or rooms with no session peers (or use the robust estimator over the floor, but never the plain mean). Keep the existing behaviour of withHint. Tests: session room 1 = 4×5 with a 1.5 m wall at 30°, room 2 square; room 2's dominantAngle must equal room 1's square axis within 1e-9. Editor floor with an unsnapped room at 12°: the re-solved room's axis must not move.

### SEAM-5 — Round-1 AR-6 only partly fixed: calibration still rescales typed ceilings, typed wall lengths and default-width openings in scanned rooms

- **Severity:** minor (reviewer: minor) · **Verdict:** confirmed
- **Where:** `floorplan/core/src/main/kotlin/com/oroplex/floorplan/core/solve/RoomOps.kt:709`

- **Problem:** scaleMeasured treats everything in a WALLS/CORNERS room as AR-measured, except an estimated ceiling and opening heights (RoomOps.kt:704-720). The editor's setCeilingHeight stores a typed (tape) height with ceilingEstimated = false (PlanEditorViewModel.kt:418), so a later FloorOps.calibrate scales it. setWallLength keeps the room a WALLS room, so a typed length is rescaled. Doors and windows added in the editor with the new round-2 defaultDoorWidth/defaultWindowWidth (PlanEditorViewModel.kt:523-537), or with widths typed in the opening dialog, get their width and offset scaled too. The Room.ceilingEstimated flag merges "typed" and "AR-measured" into one state.
- **Failure scenario:** The user tape-measures the living room ceiling and types 2.70 m. They type a tape length of 3.000 m for one wall via Set length and add a 32" door in the editor. Then they calibrate the floor from another wall (×1.03). The PDF schedule shows a ceiling of 2.78 m, the typed wall reads 3.09 m, and the door is 33". All three are values the user entered by hand, changed without notice.
- **Suggested fix:** Track where values came from: add Room.ceilingSource (MEASURED/TYPED/ESTIMATED) or a ceilingTyped flag, a per-opening `measured` flag set only by capture placement, and per-edge or per-room 'typed length' markers. Have scaleMeasured skip typed values. At minimum, warn in the calibrate dialog that typed lengths in scanned rooms will be rescaled.

- **Verifier:** FloorOps.scaleMeasured (RoomOps.kt:704-720) scales the ceiling unless room.ceilingEstimated, and always scales opening offset and width. PlanEditorViewModel.setCeilingHeight stores a typed height with ceilingEstimated = false, so a later calibrate treats it as AR-measured and scales it: 2.70 m × 1.03 = 2.78 m. setWallLength keeps captureMethod WALLS/CORNERS, so a typed edge is rescaled: 3.000 m becomes 3.09 m. addOpening uses settings.defaultDoorWidth (32") or a typed width, and that width is scaled to about 33". The CalibrateDialog gives no warning about this. The order is realistic: users type tape heights and lengths during editing and often calibrate last. The model cannot tell typed values from measured ones, so the finding is right that round-1 AR-6 is only partly fixed (MANUAL rooms, estimated ceilings, opening heights and later rooms are handled).

- **Fix notes:** Model (additive, with defaults that keep current behaviour for old JSON): `Room.ceilingTyped: Boolean = false`, set to true in PlanEditorViewModel.setCeilingHeight (ceilingEstimated stays false); `Opening.widthTyped: Boolean = false` (or `placedByCapture`), set to true in PlanEditorViewModel.addOpening and wherever the opening dialog edits width. Capture placement (RoomOps.placeOpening from capture) leaves it false. scaleMeasured: skip the ceiling when `ceilingEstimated || ceilingTyped`. For typed-width openings, scale the opening's centre position (center' = center·f, offset' = center' − width/2, clamped to the edge) and keep the width. Typed wall lengths: per-edge markers are fragile under insert/delete corner. The pragmatic fix is a `typedEdges` set kept by RoomOps edits, re-applying RoomOps.setWallLength after scaling, or at minimum a CalibrateDialog notice listing scanned rooms whose lengths were typed. Update the core io JSON codec if it is hand-written, and add tests for calibrate keeping a typed ceiling, a typed door width and (if implemented) a typed edge.


### SEAM-6 — Calibration scales scanned rooms about the floor centre but leaves hand-drawn rooms in place, so manual rooms snapped to neighbours end up overlapping or with a gap

- **Severity:** minor (reviewer: minor) · **Verdict:** confirmed
- **Where:** `floorplan/core/src/main/kotlin/com/oroplex/floorplan/core/solve/RoomOps.kt:551`

- **Problem:** FloorOps.calibrate scales only AR-measured rooms about one origin (RoomOps.kt:547-551). MANUAL rooms keep both their size and their position. A scanned room at distance d from the origin moves by (f−1)·d, while a hand-drawn closet the user snapped against it does not move. The wall panel still says "Floor calibrated ×f" for walls of MANUAL rooms that calibration did not touch (EditorPanels.kt:255-258).
- **Failure scenario:** A floor has scanned rooms plus a hand-drawn 1.2 × 0.9 m closet, snapped to a bedroom 6 m from the floor centre. Calibrating ×1.03 moves the bedroom's shared wall about 18 cm toward the closet. The rooms now overlap by about 0.16 m², so the floor check and pre-export card show an ERROR "… overlaps … — move or snap it", and export asks "Export anyway?". With f < 1, a gap of about 18 cm opens instead, with no warning.
- **Suggested fix:** In calibrate and applyCalibration, translate MANUAL rooms with the scale without resizing them: move each by (f−1)·(centroid − origin). Alternatively, re-snap MANUAL rooms that were aligned with a scanned neighbour before calibration. Show the calibration note only for rooms it covers.

- **Verifier:** FloorOps.calibrate maps only isArMeasured rooms through scaleMeasured about one origin. MANUAL rooms are returned unchanged, so they keep both size and position. A scanned wall whose line lies d from the origin moves (f−1)·d; at f = 1.03 and d = 6 m that is 18 cm. FloorOps.snapRoom leaves one wall thickness (0.1143 m) between snapped interiors, so the actual interior overlap is (0.18 − 0.114)·L ≈ 0.08 m² for a 1.2 m closet wall, not the 0.16 m² claimed. That still exceeds FloorChecks.overlappingRooms' 0.05 m² threshold, so the ERROR and "Export anyway?" gate fire. Smaller shifts (f = 1.02, d = 4 m gives 8 cm) do not trigger the check but leave visibly doubled or thinned wall bands. With f < 1 a gap opens and the alignment check ignores drawn rooms. The "Floor calibrated ×f" note in the wall panel (EditorPanels:255-261) is shown for any room, MANUAL included. The CalibrateDialog says "All N rooms on this floor are scaled", which is also misleading.

- **Fix notes:** FloorOps.calibrate: for MANUAL rooms apply RoomOps.transform with a pure translation of (factor − 1)·(centroid − origin), so they keep their size but stay in the calibrated frame. A translation by the centroid leaves a residual of about (f−1)·half-depth (≈1.4 cm for a 0.9 m closet). For rooms that were snapped, record their aligned neighbour before scaling (WallPairs.facing with gap ≈ wallThickness, as FloorChecks does) and re-run FloorOps.snapRoom against that neighbour afterwards. Leave applyCalibration unchanged (rooms added later are placed by the user in the calibrated frame). EditorPanels wall panel: show the calibration note only when FloorOps.isArMeasured(room). CalibrateDialog: count only scanned rooms and say drawn rooms keep their size but move with the plan. Test: a closet snapped to a scanned room 6 m from the origin stays non-overlapping and aligned after calibrate ×1.03 and ×0.97.


### SEAM-7 — On a calibrated floor the mini-map draws saved session rooms in the scaled frame next to the raw live draft and camera

- **Severity:** minor (reviewer: minor) · **Verdict:** confirmed
- **Where:** `floorplan/app/src/main/java/com/oroplex/floorplan/ui/capture/MiniMap.kt:58`

- **Problem:** ui.sessionRooms comes from the stored floor (CaptureViewModel.kt:208-212). ### MATH-1 — Short walls (about 0.5 m or less) seen at an angle from the room centre are silently fitted to a fake diagonal or to the next wall; the solver then can delete a correct wall
